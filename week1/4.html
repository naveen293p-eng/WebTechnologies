Vignan’s Foundation for Science, Technology and Research (VFSTR), widely known as Vignan University, is a private deemed-to-be university located in Vadlamudi, Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh. Established in 2008 (with roots dating back to 1997), it offers diverse undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, management, science, and law.
